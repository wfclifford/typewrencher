    About Typewrencher™

    Typewrencher wrenches words.

    Type, paste, or upload a text file. Each line shows its syllable count in the margin. ~ means a word was estimated; ± means it has more than one common pronunciation.

    There is no Save, only Export! Tap the skull or refresh your browser to erase the text and filename.

    Counts and rhymes come from CMUdict, a pronunciation dictionary, plus additions for words whose syllable count varies. Words in neither list are estimated. Dictionary Options below contain the dictionary controls for syllables and rhymes and the full CMUdict license.

    Dictionary Options

    CMUdict

    More accurate than estimating from spelling. When off, all words use spelling estimates.

    Corrects words that can be said with different syllable counts, like caramel. Applies only while the dictionary is on.

    Adds a Rhymes panel under the text. Downloads about 3.5 MB the first time.

    Keeps the Rhymes panel the same height, so the text above it does not move while you type.

    Off shows only exact rhymes, like night/light. On also shows near matches that share the stressed vowel, like night/mine.

    Off hides words that share the same starting sound, like bread/red. On includes them. Applies to exact rhymes.
